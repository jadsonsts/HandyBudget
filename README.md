# 💰 HandyBudget

A clean, intuitive budgeting app that helps users understand their money at a glance. HandyBudget makes it easy to track income, expenses, categories, and whether upcoming bills fit within your monthly or weekly salary.

---

## 📱 Overview
HandyBudget gives users clarity over their financial life through:
- **Adding transactions** (income & expenses)
- **Organizing transactions** with categories
- **Checking if salary covers bills** for the month
- **Visual budget insights** using charts and color cues

**Brand palette:**
- **Teal** `#01B8AA` (primary)
- **White** `#FFFFFF`
- **Red** `#FF5050`
- **Dark Red** `#CC0000`
- **Dark Grey** `#202020`

The interface and branding follow Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines, focusing on simple shapes and a text‑free, globally recognizable identity.

---

## 🎨 Branding & Logo Direction
HandyBudget’s evolving logo explores a modern, minimalist financial identity built on:
- A **teal background** for instant brand recognition
- **White line‑based illustrations** for clarity & contrast
- **Accent red and grey tones** for balance and energy
- Variations using:
  - **Charts** (bar, line, pie)
  - **Currency symbols** ($)
  - **Dynamic geometry** representing flow, budgeting, and smart decisions

 Refined with a **liquid‑glass style** to add depth and dimensional shine.
